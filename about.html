06 FREQUENT INQUIRIES

Everything You Need To Know.

Answers to common questions regarding our bespoke custom shop builds, VIP rental fleets, battery engineering, and white-glove nationwide delivery.

Unlike mass-produced plastic carts, every Cart Craft is individually hand-assembled like a luxury automobile. We utilize robotic CNC-machined aerospace alloy chassis, marine-grade anodization, whisper-silent dual AC regenerative motors, and hand-tailored Italian marine Napa leather. Our vehicles are engineered for a 20+ year service life on private estates and championship courses.

Yes, our Bespoke Atelier program offers virtually limitless personalization. You can select exact paint codes (including metallic, satin matte, and ceramic coats matched to your supercars), hand-quilted leather patterns, 24K gold or platinum leaf emblem inlays, carbon-fiber dash finishes, bespoke Bluetooth audio acoustics, and custom monogramming.

Our rental division caters to PGA tournaments, corporate hospitality, private celebrity weddings, and luxury resort seasonal leases. We provide 2, 4, 6, and 8-passenger fleets delivered in specialized enclosed transporters, 100% battery charged, detailed to showroom standards, and accompanied by an on-site technician team for complete operational peace of mind.

Our ultra-dense lithium packs deliver up to 120 km (75 miles) on a single charge under typical estate driving conditions. Equipped with an onboard rapid-charge smart management system, our carts reach 80% charge in approximately 2.5 hours using any standard 110V/220V household outlet. The lithium cells are rated for over 3,500 charge cycles and backed by a comprehensive 5-year warranty.

Yes, our carts can be configured to meet full US DOT FMVSS 500 Low-Speed Vehicle (LSV) compliance. This package includes a 17-digit VIN, automotive three-point seat belts, DOT-approved safety glass windshield with wiper, dual side mirrors, turn signals, high/low beam LED headlamps, horn, and rearview camera, permitting legal driving on roads with speed limits up to 35 mph.

We coordinate enclosed, climate-controlled transport directly to your driveway, club dock, or estate gate. Upon arrival, our technician performs a comprehensive walk-through, hand-delivers your custom vehicle documentation, and configures your charging station. For maintenance, our mobile service fleet handles on-site diagnostics, scheduled care, and warranty servicing.
